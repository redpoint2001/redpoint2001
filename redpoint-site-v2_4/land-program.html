VACANT LAND OWNER FINANCING

Every deed begins
at a point of beginning.

In a legal description, "point of beginning" is the exact spot every boundary is measured from — the fixed mark a survey can always return to. It's also the oldest, most literal version of what REDPOINT already stands for. We originate owner-finance land deals compliantly, so developers and land sellers have a fixed point to start from.

THE OPENING

A specific niche, mostly unserved.

Vacant land is one of the few corners of real estate where owner financing isn't a workaround — it's often the only path to close, since traditional lenders rarely touch raw land. Demand for it is high. Supply of people who know how to originate it compliantly is not.

Today, that gap is mostly filled by a handful of companies running the process in-house, for their own subdivisions only. REDPOINT originates it as a standalone service — available to any developer or land seller, not just one with its own origination team.

HOW IT WORKS

You send the deal. We run the process.

Send us the property, the terms, and your buyer's information. REDPOINT originates the file end to end — no origination staff needed on your side.
