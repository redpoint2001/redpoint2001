LOAN PROGRAMS

Three borrower types, three sets of programs. Find yours below.

SEC. 01 — RESIDENTIAL

Homeowners

Purchase and refinance loans for the home you live in — built around a rate and term you understand before you sign anything.

  • PurchaseFixed and adjustable-rate financing for buying a primary residence.
  • Rate & term refinanceLower your rate or change your loan term without pulling cash out.
  • Cash-out refinanceAccess home equity for renovation, debt payoff, or other use.
  • First-time buyerGuidance through documentation and down payment options for first purchases.
SEC. 02 — INVESTMENT

Investors

Financing that underwrites the property and the deal — not only your personal W-2 income.

  • Rental property loansPurchase or refinance a single rental property.
  • DSCR loansQualification based on the property's rental income rather than personal income documentation.
  • Portfolio financingFinancing structured across multiple investment properties held by one borrower or entity.
  • Fix-and-flipShort-term financing for value-add and resale projects.
SEC. 03 — CONSTRUCTION

Contractors

Draw schedules and disbursement built around your actual build timeline.

  • Construction-to-permanentOne closing covers the build and converts to a permanent loan once complete.
  • Ground-up constructionFinancing for new builds on owned or acquired lots.
  • Renovation & rehabFinancing for substantial renovation or rehabilitation projects.
  • Draw managementDisbursements released against inspection milestones tied to your build schedule.
