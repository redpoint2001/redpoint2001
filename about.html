ABOUT REDPOINT

A fixed reference point
in a variable market.

In surveying, a "redpoint" is a benchmark — a known, fixed elevation everything else is measured against. We built the name around that idea on purpose: financing should give you something stable to work from, even when rates, timelines, and property conditions are moving underneath you.

WHAT WE DO

We originate loans. That's it.

REDPOINT Mortgage PLLC is a mortgage loan origination company. We work with homeowners buying or refinancing a primary residence, investors financing rental and portfolio property, and contractors financing new construction and construction-to-permanent builds.

We don't service loans long-term or manage deposits — our job is getting your file from application to a clean, funded closing, with terms that were clear from the first conversation.

HOW WE WORK

Underwritten for the borrower, not a template.

A W-2 homeowner, a landlord with six units, and a contractor mid-build don't fit the same form. We route each loan to the program built for that borrower — residential, investment (including asset- and rental-income-based options), or construction draw schedules tied to your build timeline.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

Standards we hold on every file.

  • Plain termsRate, points, and fees explained before you lock — not buried in closing disclosures.
  • Status visibilityYou'll know what stage your file is in and what's needed next, at every step.
  • Program fitWe tell you when a program isn't a fit rather than force a square file into a round loan.
  • Direct accessA named point of contact for your file, not a rotating queue.
